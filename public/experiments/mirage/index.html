Your image never leaves this device. After the model is saved, running it makes no network requests.

An image longer than 1600 px on its long side is scaled down in this browser before it is read. The whole frame stays visible.

The model is stored in Cache Storage (mirage-model). The page, worker, and runtime are in the service worker cache (mirage-shell). To remove them, use DevTools, Application, Storage, Clear site data.

The service worker records fetches from this page and from the vision worker. The page also records resource timing and wraps fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, and sendBeacon.
