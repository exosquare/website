435 parameters. Position encodes signed value.

Each circle is one connection weight. Each outlined diamond is one bias. There are 400 circles and 35 diamonds. Direction comes only from the index. Distance is R times (0.60 plus 0.40 times the value divided by 16 plus the absolute value). Negative 16 sits at 0.40R, zero at 0.60R, and positive 16 at 0.80R.

Large magnitudes sit closer together. That crowding is not equality and not a score. The same directions and scale are used for every preset. Reordering hidden units can change this shape without changing behaviour.

Scores describe the world before a tick. The court shows the world after that tick.

New serve starts the serve chosen under Game settings, from tick 0. G0 to G3 are fixed starts. Local draws a new serve and records its seed in this tab. It has no official score.

Pause stops the clock, or starts it again. Step plays one tick and stays paused. A finished serve does not take another step.

Stay, Up, and Down show a rounded display of the score for the world before the tick. The exact raw score and the exact q stay in the disclosure, each number kept whole. q is floor of raw score times 1,000,000 plus 0.5. The highest q wins. A tie uses the lowest index. Scores are not probabilities. The chosen action is separate. The court shows the world after the tick.

The right paddle is a frozen baseline, not a contestant. Stay always holds. Random chooses from its own stream and keeps drawing until the draw is accepted. Intervention
Local fork

No comparison yet. Run comparison freezes this world, opponent, and random stream, then plays the parent and one muted fork forward.

Layer and unit choose the highlight. H1 unit n uses flat indices 112 plus 16n through 127 plus 16n. H2 unit n uses 384 plus 3n through 386 plus 3n. Input and output have no outgoing mute.

Parent demonstration is the authored set of 435 numbers. Local fork is one mute of the selected hidden unit. Mute copies that parent and sets the selected outgoing weights to 0. Incoming weights and every bias stay. If they were already 0, the changed count is 0 and that is not an error.

Reset weights restores the demonstration parent and clears the fork. The ball stays where it is.

Run comparison copies the full checkpoint, including both controllers and the random counters, then runs the parent and the fork to the end with that same opponent. Changing the opponent afterwards does not alter the captured run. Reset comparison restores that checkpoint. It does not by itself restore weights.
